## Who they are

Clerk in the harbourmaster's office, twenty-six, quick and dry-humoured. To Ilse she is an informant and something close to a friend: she sells the patrol times for a mark a night and drinks with Ilse after a good run.

## What they want

Now: to get her brother Anders out of the Brenner debt before the guild takes it out of his hands. Underneath: to stop being the person everyone pays and nobody protects.

## Why it is justified to them

Ilse pays a mark a night and keeps the profit. The guild pays off Anders's whole debt for one route and one name. Ilse would do the same; she has said so.

## What they know

The Kittiwake's landing nights and the north-quay timings. That Ilse keeps a ledger. Not that Ilse knows about Thale's cousin.

## What they will and will not do

Will lie to Ilse's face and keep selling her timings. Will not testify in front of her. Would warn Ilse once, quietly, if Anders were already free.

## Toward the persona

Likes her and resents her. Wants Ilse to survive this, somewhere else.

## The lie and the truth

The lie: "Thale doubled the south patrol, the north quay is clear at two." The truth: she sold tonight's landing to the Brenner guild, who passed it to Thale to remove a rival. She keeps the lie up by being specific and a little bored, and by complaining about her own pay.

## Current move

Waiting at the Anchor for the guild's man to pay her the second half.

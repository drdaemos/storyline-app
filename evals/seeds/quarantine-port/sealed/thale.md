## Who they are

Captain of the customs watch, forty-five, neat, patient, polite. To Ilse: the man whose job is to stop her, and who has been too slow to do it until now.

## What they want

Now: a seizure big enough to put his name before the harbour board when the harbourmaster retires in spring. Underneath: to be the man who held the city during the quarantine, whatever it cost.

## Why it is justified to them

Every unlicensed cargo can carry the cholera in. Smugglers sell medicine to the rich and call it mercy. He is the only one keeping the gate shut.

## What they know

The Kittiwake's name and Ilse's. That someone in the harbourmaster's office sells timings. That his cousin's brandy came through under his seal, and that only three people know.

## What they will and will not do

Will not take a bribe in front of his men. Will not be seen to back down. Will deal in private if the leverage is real and proven, and will remember who made him do it. Will not hurt a prisoner himself.

## Toward the persona

Respects her seamanship, despises her prices. Sees her as a rung, not an enemy.

## Current move

On the Kittiwake's deck with two men, about to search the floor.

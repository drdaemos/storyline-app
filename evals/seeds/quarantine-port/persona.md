Thirty-eight, widow of a fisherman, runs the Kittiwake alone or with a hired hand. Knows the bay's shoals and tides better than the watch does. Good at reading people, bad at trusting them, worse at walking away from money. Carries a folding knife and a ledger of who owes her. Sells to apothecaries first and to the rich when the apothecaries cannot pay.

Holds: twelve tins of quinine and six bottles of laudanum under the Kittiwake's false floor. Owes: forty marks to her supplier, Brenner. Knows: Thale's cousin brought a cargo of unlicensed brandy through the quarantine in September, under Thale's own seal.

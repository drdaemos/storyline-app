# The north quay

The fog sat on the harbour like wet wool, and the Kittiwake came in under it with her lamp dark. Ilse brought her alongside the north quay at a quarter past two, the hour Vera had sold her, and listened. Water against stone. A gull. Nothing else.

She had the first two tins of quinine in her coat when the lantern opened on the quay above her.

"Miss Marr." Captain Thale did not raise his voice. He did not need to; two of his men stood behind him with their hands on their belts. "Late for fishing."

"Mending a line, Captain."

"In the dark." He stepped down onto the Kittiwake's deck as if it were his own, and the boat dipped under him. "Your manifest. And open the floor."

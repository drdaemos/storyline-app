## The user's request

"Let me play a smuggler running medicine into a port under cholera quarantine. Grim, morally dirty, people with their own reasons. I don't want it to go easy on me."

## Premise

Lübeck-like Baltic port, autumn 1892. The city is under quarantine; nothing comes in without customs clearance, and quinine and laudanum sell for ten times their price. Ilse Marr runs them in on her boat, the Kittiwake, from a supplier across the bay.

## Threads in play

- Captain Thale of the customs watch is closing in on the Kittiwake.
- The north-quay patrol times Ilse buys from Vera at the harbourmaster's office.
- Ilse owes her supplier for the last cargo, which she has not sold yet.

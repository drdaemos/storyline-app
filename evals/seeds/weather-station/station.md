Three green huts on a boardwalk 200 m above the jetty: main hut (desk, radio, galley), bunk room, store and generator shed. Instrument mast behind the huts. Barograph on the main hut wall records pressure on a paper drum.

Radio: HF set with a scheduled call to the ship at 08:00 and 20:00. The ship leaves for the ice at dawn on day one and is out of reliable contact for weeks after.

Island: about 30 km long, treeless, tussock and black rock. A track runs north along the coast to an old hut at Seal Bay. Westerlies most days; storms come through every few days with little warning.

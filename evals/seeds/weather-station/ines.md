Forty-four, Hungarian-Australian, eleventh season on remote stations. Methodical to the point of rudeness; checks things twice and says so. Hates being managed. Was once blamed for a colleague's data error and has never quite let it go, so Kerr's faked readings hit a nerve.

Small and compact, moves economically, keeps her hands in her pockets when she is thinking.

Wants to do this season cleanly and go home to her sister's new baby. Does not entirely trust Tomasz, whom she met on the ship.

Voice: "I don't care why. I care what's true on the page."

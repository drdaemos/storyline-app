## The user's request

"A slow-burn mystery on a remote subantarctic weather station: two technicians arrive to relieve a third who isn't there, and his logbook doesn't add up. Keep it grounded, nothing supernatural."

## Intentions

Close third person on Ines, past tense. Plain, concrete prose; the weather is a pressure on the characters, not scenery. Tension comes from small wrong details and from what Ines and Tomasz choose not to say to each other. No supernatural explanation; the answer is human.

## Arc

Kerr faked nine days of readings to cover time he spent elsewhere on the island. What he found there, and why he hid it, drives the second half. Ines and Tomasz disagree about reporting him.

## Next scenes

1. First night: Ines compares the barograph trace with the log; Tomasz wants to search before dark, she wants to radio the ship first.
2. The ship does not answer clearly; they search the north track and find Kerr's camera tripod set up facing an empty bay.

## Open

Whether Kerr is alive is not decided. Put the options to the user before the search scene reveals anything.

## The oracle

Some outcomes are not yours to choose. Use roll when an outcome is contested and the cast sheets do not settle it, such as whether the persona's lie holds against someone with reason to doubt it, and for questions about the world that you would otherwise answer to suit the persona, such as whether the guard is awake.

Set the odds from the fiction before you roll: almost certain, likely, even, unlikely or small chance. Ask the question so that yes and no both lead somewhere. Then narrate what came up. Never roll again for the same question, and never turn a no into a yes through the narration. A strong yes or strong no goes further than the question asked: the lie is believed and the guard confides something, or the lie is caught and the guard calls for others.

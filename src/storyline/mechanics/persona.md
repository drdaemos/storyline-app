## The persona

The user plays one character in this story, the persona. persona.md is the persona's sheet: who they are, what they want, what they are good and bad at, what they carry, their injuries and debts, the people they know and what they have learned. It belongs to the user and they can see it. Keep it current after every scene, recording what the story established and nothing you would like to be true: a new piece of the persona's past is something to propose at the table, not to write in.

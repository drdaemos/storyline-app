## Clocks

A clock tracks something that builds over time: a plan coming together, a threat closing in, suspicion growing, a debt falling due, a ritual nearing completion. Create one with set_clock, with 4 segments for something close, 6 for something steady and 8 for something slow, and say what happens when it fills. A clock is visible when the persona would see it coming, like a guard's growing suspicion; keep it hidden when it belongs to a character's secret plan. The user sees visible clocks beside the conversation, and only the number of hidden ones.

Tick a clock with tick_clock whenever something advances it, with the reason: time passing offscreen, the persona's action or inaction, a roll. Clocks keep ticking when the persona is busy elsewhere; that is how the world moves without them. When a clock fills, what it promised happens in the next scene, and then you remove it or start the next one. Do not quietly let a clock stall because filling it would hurt the persona.

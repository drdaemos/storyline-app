---

A cast sheet is a commitment. It is written before the character shares a scene with the persona, so what they want is settled before the persona's charm, need or argument can bend it.

## What to do

1. Read persona.md, plan.md, the lore and the other cast sheets the character touches.
2. Generate several possibilities privately and set aside the obvious one: the mentor with a secret, the villain who wants power for its own sake, the love interest who exists to love the persona. Give the character a life that does not revolve around the persona.
3. Write the sheet into sealed/<name>.md with a summary and keywords header, in these sections:
   - **Who they are:** the face they show, and what the persona can know about them.
   - **What they want:** something concrete now, and what lies under it.
   - **Why it is justified to them:** the account in which they are right. Even a monster has one.
   - **What they know:** about the persona, about the others, and what they have wrong.
   - **What they will and will not do:** their own limits, which are not the story's bounds. Most people have a line; some do not.
   - **Toward the persona:** how they see the persona and what they want from them.
   - **Current move:** what they are doing right now, offscreen.
4. For a deceiver, write both the lie and the truth: what they tell people, what is really so, and how they keep the lie up when pressed. A good lie is mostly true.
5. When the character's plan is a threat that unfolds over time, give it a clock, hidden unless the persona would see it coming.
6. Make the cast's agendas pull against each other as well as against the persona, so the world has its own conflicts for the persona to walk into.
7. At the table, tell the user only what the trust level for cast design says: who the character is to the persona and the part they play, never what the sheet hides.

Update the sheet when the character learns something, changes their mind for a reason the story gave them, or makes a move. A character who changes without a reason has broken their sheet.

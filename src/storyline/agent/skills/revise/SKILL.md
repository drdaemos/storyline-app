Revision changes what is there with as little resending as possible.

## What to do

1. Work out what the feedback asks for. If it could mean two things, say how you read it before editing.
2. Prefer targeted edits. Use edit_script with a short excerpt copied exactly that occurs only once; read_script first if you need the exact text. Rewrite a whole passage only when the feedback calls for it.
3. If the feedback applies beyond the passage, record it. Preferences about the prose, such as plainer metaphors or more dialogue, go in style.md. Preferences about how you work, such as asking before a death or moving faster, go in collaboration.md, with the trust level that follows from them. Follow it in everything that comes after.
4. If the revision changes a fact, update the lore file that holds it, and plan.md if the direction changes.
5. Say briefly what you changed and what you would do about similar passages, and end on the one decision you need, if there is one.

A correction or sign of frustration in a kind of decision moves that kind down a level in collaboration.md, and you say so.

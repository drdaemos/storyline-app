An outline gives the writing a direction without fixing every step. Propose it only when the premise, main characters and tone are settled enough to support it.

## What to do

1. Read plan.md and the lore. The outline has to serve the user's request above everything else.
2. Propose the arc in broad strokes and the next few scenes in more detail. For each scene say what it is for: what the characters want, what stands in their way, and what will be different when it ends. Explain why the structure is shaped this way and what alternatives you set aside, such as a different order of reveals or a different midpoint.
3. Plot turns are usually a decision for the user: follow their trust level and bring options for the turns that matter, with your recommendation.
4. Write the outline into plan.md only once the user has agreed. Update it in small pieces rather than rewriting the whole file.
5. Turn the agreed scenes into agenda items with set_agenda, keeping settled items and their outcomes. From here the agenda is mostly scenes.

End on the one decision you need, usually whether the outline is right to start writing from.

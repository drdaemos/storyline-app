name: develop-element
description: Use when working out one element of the story into a decision, such as the setting, a character, the central conflict, a twist, the voice or the tone. Produces a few genuinely different options with reasoning, or follows the trust level for that kind.
---

Work on one element per turn. If the user's message touches several, say which you are taking and leave the others on the agenda.

## What to do

1. Check collaboration.md for the trust level of this kind of decision, and read the lore and plan that bear on the element.
2. Generate several possibilities privately and judge how typical each one is. The first ideas are the ones every story gets; the common choice is worth naming so you can set it aside on purpose.
3. If the level is options, bring two or three genuinely different ones, including at least one non-obvious, each with what it does for the story. Say which you dropped and why. Recommend one and say why.
4. If the level is propose, bring your recommendation and its reasoning and wait for a yes. If it is decide and tell, decide and say what you decided so the user can overrule it. If it is decide quietly, decide. Follow the level rather than the default of bringing options.
5. Keep proposals out of lore until the user accepts them. After they decide, write the decision into the right lore file in a small piece: the character's own file, the setting's file, or plan.md for direction. Then settle the agenda item with the outcome in a few words.
6. Note the user's reaction in collaboration.md: accepted as offered, changed, or rejected, and what that suggests about the level.

End on the one decision you need next.
